The Voice Behind It

Pete D. Jenkins

Grimdark, written from the conviction that darkness only earns its keep when it knows exactly what it costs.

Born in New York and raised in Wales, he's a dual national — a fact he finds completely unremarkable and which everyone else insists on finding fascinating. The North of his books is Norse on purpose and to the bone: the gods keep their right names, the seiðr keeps its right rules, and the prophecy has a name, because an invented world deserves real bones and not set‑dressing borrowed from somewhere warmer.

Náströnd is the first book of the Vargöld trilogy. Blóðniðr, the second, is being hauled up out of the cold as fast as the work allows. Harrow and They Fear the Thaw came before it, and taught him most of what he knows about how much a reader can carry before they put the book down — and how to make sure they don't.

He does not write happy endings. He writes true ones. There's a difference, and the North insists on it.
